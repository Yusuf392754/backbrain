# BackBrain by Yusuf competition presentation guide

## Choose the package

- iPhone 15 with Safari: `packages/backbrain-iphone-safari.zip`
- Lenovo computer with Chrome: `packages/backbrain-lenovo-chrome.zip`

Both packages are self-contained and do not require a website URL, API key, account, or internet connection after transfer.

## Presentation flow

1. Open BackBrain on the chosen device.
2. Start on the home screen and introduce it as a memory assistant for a 13-year-old.
3. Show Yusuf’s people: Aakash, Rishan, Dr. Baumfolk, Syed, Afsheen, Yusra, and Yunus.
4. Open the calendar and show studying, swim practice, and taekwondo.
5. Open **You** and drag, orbit, zoom, and select the connected knowledge-graph nodes.
6. Open a topic card and show how details are organized.
7. Switch between light and dark themes.
8. Open **Settings** and turn **mock data** on. This intentionally loads the personalized presentation records. Press **Ask** or **Start listening**; the offline presentation build waits eight seconds and speaks the personalized best-friends response.

## Accurate explanation

The offline package is a reliable presentation build. It demonstrates the interface and interaction model without external services. The connected architecture is prepared for Supabase authentication and encrypted records, a server-side OpenAI route, and Stripe billing, but full zero-knowledge E2EE, live speech intent extraction, multi-device synchronization, and background notifications are not claims of the offline package.

Do not enter real passwords, medical records, payment information, or sensitive constituent information.
